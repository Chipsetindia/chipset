CHIPSET V34 — INVESTMENT AGREEMENT FINAL HARDENING

WHAT THIS VERSION ADDS
1. A customer must acknowledge plan details + risk disclosure before submitting payment proof.
2. After an investment payment is approved by an admin, Supabase automatically creates an immutable agreement snapshot.
3. Agreement snapshot contains customer details, plan, investment amount, return/earning terms, earning periods, maturity, payment ID, UTR, approval time, disclaimer, disclosure and risk notice.
4. Customer dashboard shows Investment Agreements only after approval.
5. Customer can open the agreement and use PRINT / SAVE AS PDF.
6. Customer RLS allows a customer to read only their own agreements. No client insert/update/delete policy is provided.
7. Customer acknowledgement timestamp + terms version are recorded for new payments.
7. Existing approved investments are backfilled when the V32 SQL is run.

SUPABASE STEP
Run these files once in Supabase SQL Editor:
  supabase/V32-INVESTMENT-AGREEMENTS.sql
  supabase/V34-FINAL-AGREEMENT-HARDENING.sql

RUN IT AFTER THE EXISTING FINAL INVESTMENT / DAILY-EARNING / REFERRAL MIGRATIONS.

IMPORTANT
- The agreement is a terms snapshot; later catalogue edits do not rewrite an already-approved agreement.
- Current delivery is secure dashboard access + print/save-to-PDF. Automatic email delivery is NOT enabled by this version because that requires a server-side email provider/Edge Function.
- Have the final agreement wording reviewed by qualified Indian legal/compliance counsel before using it as a legally binding investment contract.
