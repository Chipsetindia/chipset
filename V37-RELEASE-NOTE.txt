CHIPSET V37 — Principal Lock vs Investment Term

Business rule:
- Principal lock = first 30 days only.
- Investment/earning term remains the approved plan term (weekly 52 periods = up to 364 days).
- Daily interest is credited independently throughout the active investment term.
- Auto-reinvestment occurs at the end of the full investment term, not at day 30.
- Principal withdrawal is eligible after the 30-day lock subject to applicable withdrawal controls/process.
